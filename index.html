Capybara Cafe – Privacy Policy

Developer: Bovii Studio · Contact: bovii.studio@gmail.com · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Capybara Cafe (Android, package com.boviistudio.capybaracafe) collects, why, and what choices you have. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
A random player ID created by the game on your device (not linked to your real identity).To keep your save and team membership, and to count sessions in anonymous statistics.
Gameplay events: progress (venue, play time), tutorial steps, ads watched, purchases made, sessions.To understand where players get stuck and to improve and balance the game.
Device information: phone model, Android version, memory, screen size, language, time zone, app version.To fix bugs and keep the game running smoothly on all phones.
Country, estimated from your connection when statistics are received. Your IP address is not stored.Regional statistics.
Team Hotel (optional online feature): the player name and team name you choose, your floor design, donations and team messages such as reports.To show your floor and team to your teammates. An anonymous account is created automatically when you open the Team Hotel; no sign-up form is needed.
Ad revenue information reported by Google AdMob for ads you watch (amount and ad network, not who you are).To understand how the free game is funded.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change this choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. To verify that a purchase is genuine and to restore your purchases, the purchase receipt is checked through RevenueCat, which receives a purchase token and an anonymous ID only.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase (database hosting, servers in the EU – Frankfurt): anonymous statistics and Team Hotel data.
  • Google AdMob: rewarded advertising.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat: in-app purchases.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep data

Anonymous statistics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted or aggregated. Team Hotel data is kept while your team exists. Your game save is stored on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

6. Your choices and rights

You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the game. To ask for a copy or deletion of the data linked to your player ID or Team Hotel name, email bovii.studio@gmail.com with your player name; we will reply within 30 days.

7. Children

Capybara Cafe is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Team Hotel, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
